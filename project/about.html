Our Vision

MPD Hiking Adventures was born from a simple idea: to make the beauty of the hills surrounding Mar del Plata more accessible to everyone. Founded by a group of friends who shared a deep passion for hiking, the company started as a small initiative to guide locals through nearby trails, many of which were either unknown or difficult to navigate without experience. What began as weekend outings soon evolved into a dedicated service when we realized how many people wanted to explore nature but didn't know where to begin.

Over time, the founders decided to create a specialized team made up of trained guides, outdoor enthusiasts, and safety experts. Our goal was to provide a reliable and professional experience while keeping the warmth and personal attention of a small local business. We worked closely with local communities, landowners, and environmental groups to design safe routes that highlight the region's unique landscapes.

Today, MPD Hiking Adventures continues to grow while staying true to its origins. We remain committed to offering high-quality guided hikes that combine adventure, education, and respect for nature. Whether you're from Mar del Plata or visiting for the first time, we invite you to explore the hills with us and create memories that will last a lifetime.

Common Questions (FAQ)

1. What types of hikes do you offer?

We offer a wide range of guided hikes, from beginner-friendly walks to more challenging full-day adventures. Each route is designed to showcase the natural beauty of the hills near Mar del Plata, with options for families, groups, and experienced hikers.

2. Do I need any previous hiking experience?

Not at all! Many of our hikes are suitable for beginners. Our guides will help you choose the route that best matches your fitness level and experience.

3. What should I bring on a hike?

We recommend comfortable clothing, sturdy footwear, water, sunscreen, and a small backpack. Depending on the hike, our team may suggest additional items such as snacks or a light jacket.

4. Are your guides certified?

Yes. All of our guides are trained in outdoor navigation, local geography, and first aid to ensure your safety and enjoyment throughout the entire experience.

5. How do I book a hike?

You can book directly through our website, by sending us a message on social media, or by contacting us via email. We usually recommend booking at least a few days in advance to secure your spot.

6. Do you offer private or group tours?

Yes! We provide private tours for individuals, families, and groups. If you're planning a team-building event or a special outing, we can customize a hiking experience just for you.
