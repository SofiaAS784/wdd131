We are always looking for new hikers and trainers

You don't need to be a professional. Keep reading to learn the main steps to be part of our great team.

How can I apply?

• Send us your CV or a short personal introduction.

• Include your hiking experience and any training or certifications.

• We'll contact you to schedule an informal interview and a short trial hike.

• If selected, you'll join our training program before guiding official groups.

What are the requirements?

• Basic experience with hiking, trekking, or outdoor activities.

• Good physical condition and ability to walk long distances.

• Knowledge of local trails and landscapes (or willingness to learn).

• Strong communication skills and a friendly attitude towards visitors.

• Safety awareness and responsibility during outdoor activities.

• First Aid certification is a plus (or commitment to obtain one).

What are the benefits of joining our team?

• Opportunity to work outdoors and explore the hills around Mar del Plata and the region.

• Flexible schedules and seasonal work options.

• Professional development in outdoor guiding and safety skills.

• Being part of a passionate, friendly, and supportive local team.

• Potential to grow into specialized or leadership roles within the company.

What type of preparation am I going to get?

• Orientation about MPD Hiking Adventures values, safety protocols, and trail guidelines.

• Hands-on training on our most common hiking routes.

• Instruction in group management and emergency handling.

• Optional workshops on navigation, map reading, and outdoor ecology.

• Continuous feedback and support from experienced guides.

We are waiting for you!
